## TRAIN05 occupancy-distributed restart

User intention: "按照这个东西预热启动训练" and "尽快启动训练，效率高一些". Scope is a
new four-rank fork from the pinned TRAIN01 step10027008 bundles. Preserve model, Adam and replay;
replace synchronized fresh-environment startup with independent live-state occupancy sampling;
discard every warmup sample and begin the unchanged40-update production loop only after all
environments have accepted a real in-game state. The old stopped runs and source snapshots must
remain unchanged.

For a frozen policy/search trajectory `tau=(X_0,...,X_{L-1})`, `L<=H=6000`, each attempt draws
`A` uniformly from `0,...,H-1` and accepts iff `A<L`. Therefore
`P(tau,A=t | A<L)=P(tau)/E[L]` for `t<L`, and the accepted state distribution is
`E[sum_t 1{X_t in B}]/E[L]`, the renewal-reward random-decision-time occupancy measure. This is
not exact recovery of the missing old environment frontier and assumes a frozen transition kernel
during warmup. The finite fail-closed budget is131072 real decisions per unaccepted environment.
For any sequence of rejected episode lengths, the probability of remaining unaccepted after B
real decisions is at most `exp(-B/H)`; a union bound over384 environments is below`1.3e-7`.
Conditioning on finishing within a finite budget is therefore not literally zero bias, even though
the bound is small.

`StartupOccupancyWrapper` implements the rejection sampler and pauses accepted environments while
the remaining environments advance. Paused environments are excluded from upper rules, combat
search and combat sample construction. The startup boundary restores the formal global step,
leaves replay cursor/size/total-added unchanged, performs no optimizer update, resets the rollout
buffer and clears completed/pending combat roots, reasoning samples, episode buffers and logger
accumulators. `VecMonitor` episode length and return are restored to the accepted partial episode,
so the first formal completion has correct monitoring state. The production override retains rule
upper,20M target,40 NOSL updates, strict Full-Gumbel128, replay and all original batch settings; its
only startup change is `--startup-occupancy-budget 131072`.
