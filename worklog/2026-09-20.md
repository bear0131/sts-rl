### A20 upper-policy exploration: survival and encounter coverage

User intention: “别管 A0 了，请你立刻开始调 A20”, inspect many bad cases and
“多并行试验一些大不相同的上层策略”. This isolated candidate deliberately
prioritizes immediate encounter coverage, viable Act 1 damage, Act 2 area damage
and damage prevention, then boss scaling. Scope: one public-state upper-policy
library plus deterministic contract tests; no lower-policy, action/observation,
simulator, model-parameter or CLI change. Root owns shared CLI registration,
fixed-combat 128-game A20 development and independent random-seed confirmation.

Acceptance: legal deterministic decisions through DecisionStrategy, explicit
unknown-screen fallback, meaningful regressions for early damage vs speculative
powers, area damage/defense coverage, survivable routes, safe potion buying and
rest/upgrade tradeoffs. Source baseline f330921; branch
`codex/a20-upper-survival-20260920`. This is an experimental challenger and no
whole-run strength claim will be made from synthetic tests.

Implementation (FRONT01 candidate, this section accompanies current commit):
`SurvivalUpperStrategy(fallback=None, *, trace=None)` in
`rl/evaluation/baselines/overworld_survival.py`, version
`ironclad-overworld-survival-v1`. It recomputes frontload, immediate AoE, good
block, Weak, Strength, exhaust and draw coverage from the public deck. Rewards
repair role deficits first; engines require usable support, duplicated plain
attacks lose marginal value, and the A20 Ascender's Bane makes Clash undesirable.
Explicit combinations include Power Through/Second Wind, exhaust powers,
Strength/multihit/Reaper, Barricade/Entrench, and status payoffs. Campfire and
boss-relic choices put a larger price on A20 survival resources. The public map
planner propagates a coarse HP risk budget, so an intervening rest changes the
value of otherwise identical repeated-elite routes. Its numbers are human
priors, not simulated encounter damage or calibrated probabilities. Shop potion
choices use the existing native-effect whitelist, actual displayed prices,
actual empty slots, and a Sozu guard; SHOP01 itself is unchanged.
