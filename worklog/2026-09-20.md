### A20 second upper candidate: prepared growth and relic hunting

Root requested a deliberately different challenger: keep the survival candidate's
coverage-based card acquisition but test aggressive relic compounding and
upgrades. Scope is one immutable `GROWTH_PROFILE` in the same library and a
small `GrowthUpperStrategy` subclass; no duplicate policy implementation, new
runner, CLI entry point, simulator change or model update. Version is
`ironclad-overworld-growth-v1`; root owns CLI registration and full A20 evaluation.

Growth grants extra elite reward only after public deck/potion readiness and
a projected HP floor pass: Act1 direct damage, Act2 damage/AoE/block plus Weak
or a potion, Act3 AoE/block plus scaling. The map approximation consumes one
potion reserve per projected elite; a lone potion cannot justify every future
elite. Black Star adds growth value; Preserved Insect reduces the heuristic risk
cost. Without readiness, the extra elite bonus is zero. Critical upgrades get
more priority and moderate HP can be spent on smithing, while low HP and the
boss campfire retain healing thresholds. Card reward and shop purchase scores
remain identical to survival-v1, isolating this profile's route/campfire choice.

Validation uses the same explicit native build and all-overworld pytest command
as the preceding section:130 passed,2 existing strict SHOP01 xfails in0.52s.
New behavior tests cover prepared vs unprepared elite risk, low-HP recovery,
consumable potion readiness, campfire/boss distinctions, immutable configuration,
and exact shared reward ranks. Both strategy classes run native A20 legality
and bounded existing-runner smoke. A one-off frozen-v1 comparison loaded source
from df37566 and compared every upper decision during three existing-runner
A20 episodes (seeds20200920..20200922,3000actions/30seconds):268 upper decisions
had identical action/source/diagnostics, all three ended naturally in death.
This checks unchanged survival behavior on those observed states, not universal
equivalence or win-rate strength. Ignored receipt:
`runs/local/a20-survival-growth/default-equivalence.json`.

Baseline bad-case evidence supplied by the sibling reviewer makes this a useful
contrast rather than a claimed improvement: many Act1 deaths lack block/draw,
and most dying elite entries were forced by earlier map choices. Survival-v1's
future HP propagation addresses path commitment; the growth experiment asks
whether prepared extra relics can repay a larger risk budget. Fixed128 A20 and
independent-seed confirmation remain pending. This section accompanies current
commit on `codex/a20-upper-survival-20260920`.
