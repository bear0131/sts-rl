## VAL03 HP-order threshold metric diagnosis

User intention: "清理完之后调查一下为什么这个比例一直在 10% 左右，可能是什么原因". The

The collector in `rl/algorithms/accumulating_ppo.py` increments the denominator for every
`combat_root` in a naturally completed fight with finite prediction/current HP. It increments the
numerator only when `estimated_value > current_hp and current_hp >= final_hp`. Thus the denominator
is not conditioned on non-healing roots. `estimated_value` is the pre-search policy value; every
root and value-only boundary in one fight receives the same normalized terminal-HP target. The
value heads in both transformer and MLP combat policies end in an unbounded linear scalar, are
trained by MSE, and have no `predicted_value <= current_hp` constraint. Distributed reduction is
not the cause: rank match/count means are reduced together and the global ratio is recomputed.
