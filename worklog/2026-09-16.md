
### Runtime evaluation semantics and history audit

Findings: StSMetricsCallback consumes completed training episodes from infos, keeps per-rank deque(maxlen=metrics_window), default100, and records means at rollout end. distributed_sb3 aggregates game metrics by rank mean after learning and publishes once per collection/update cycle. Current fourfold preset96env ×128steps ×4ranks gives49152 global transitions per collection;40 learner updates do not mean40 new evaluation batches. Windows may overlap and include trajectories crossing policy updates; incomplete windows are averaged by rank, not weighted by episode count. Explicit rl.evaluation.eval defaults to100 episodes but is not invoked by the trainer.

### Rollout boundaries, hard truncation, and statistical bias

User follow-up: “现在 rollout 是有什么截断机制吗？……train 时候的 bias 还是 runtime eval 是不是都会不准”。Read-only audit on3c643a7: distinguished128-step collection boundary from6000-step episode TimeLimit and dead-screen truncation. The collector retains live observations/environment and NOSL pending fights across ordinary rollout boundaries; completed fights receive actual final-HP labels. PPO bootstraps at rollout end. Search budget128/depth0 is a separate planning boundary, not a live episode reset.

Concrete caveats: TimeLimit/dead_screen causes autoreset; StSMetricsCallback does not filter or separately count these outcomes, so unfinished non-wins enter rolling win-rate as0 and floor metrics use reached floor. NOSL discards the interrupted fight's pending samples, a potential selection bias if truncation is material; earlier completed fights remain usable. Hierarchical returns, when enabled (inherited from checkpoint unless overridden), omit the unresolved overworld macro transition crossing a collection boundary (NaN filtered by PPO), existing TODO F11; NOSL combat pending retention does not fix this separate upper-level loss. Resume intentionally restarts simulators/discards unfinished fights, unlike ordinary collection boundaries. Existing pending_combat_roots/incomplete_combat_roots and hierarchical/unresolved_overworld_samples can diagnose sample behavior but do not supply episode truncation rates.

Validation: inspected actual distributed environment wrappers, grouped worker autoreset, collector/callback/update filters, hierarchical returns and evaluation outcome classifier; no training executed or remote logs queried. Thus truncation mechanisms and potential bias are confirmed statically, but production incidence and bias magnitude remain unmeasured. No code/config changes or new implementation task.
