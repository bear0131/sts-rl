### Value-target mean replay-stat audit

User intention: quickly determine whether `value_target_mean` is computed over
the entire replay pool when replay is enabled.

Scope: traced `rl/algorithms/accumulating_ppo.py`,
`rl/training/distributed_sb3.py`, and `rl/algorithms/nosl_replay.py` without
changing runtime behavior. The statistic is accumulated from each sampled
training batch (`value_targets` in `_train_nosl_is_az`), with
`metric_count` equal to the number of sampled roots. Replay preparation only
appends roots and reports `replay_size`; it does not scan `value_targets` for a
pool mean. `sample_indices()` draws at most `min(batch_size, replay.size)`
entries without replacement per inner update. In distributed mode sums/counts
are reduced across ranks, and repeated inner updates are combined with
`metric_count` weighting, so the logged value is the mean of all roots actually
used by those updates, not the whole ring contents.

For the current replay preset (`batch_size=98304`, `capacity=524288`,
`max_old_fraction=0.90`), a full pool contributes only the sampled batch per
inner update (about 18.75% of the pool before the old/current stratification),
and 40 inner updates produce a weighted mean over their sampled roots. It equals
the whole-pool mean only when the effective batch covers the current pool
(`replay.size <= batch_size`, absent replacement effects).

Verification: inspected the cited source ranges and existing replay tests;
`tests/unit/test_nosl_is_az.py::TestNOSLISAZ::test_training_samples_old_and_new_roots_from_replay`
explicitly checks `replay_sampled_roots=4` while the pool grows to 6.

### Value MAE calculation follow-up

`value_mae` is computed per sampled root as
`abs(pred_value - value_target)`, where `pred_value` is the combat actor-critic
value output and `value_target` is the sampled replay/fresh target. The local
aggregator sums these absolute errors and divides by their finite-sample count;
distributed training reduces the sums and counts across ranks. Value-only
boundaries are included in this metric because only the first nine policy
metrics are filtered by `policy_weight`; `value_target`, `pred_value`, and
`value_mae` are not filtered. This is distinct from `value_loss`, which uses
per-sample squared error.
